Who We Are

Three decades of trusted property in Sharjah

Al Arif Private Real Estate was founded by Arif Alana together with his brother Muneer Alana and family — including Ahmed Alana and other relatives — with a shared belief that renting a home should feel as personal as owning one.

From a single property, the firm has grown to a portfolio of 10–12 residential and commercial spaces spread across the Sharjah emirate, offering shops and apartments to suit every lifestyle and budget.

What has never changed is the family-run ethos: every tenant is known by name, every issue is resolved with care, and every neighbourhood is chosen with intent.
